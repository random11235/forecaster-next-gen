# Capacity belongs to the team, not the milestone

Capacity is typed for the team: entries of a whole percentage of its usual, for a date range or from a date onward, for any reason (leave, an incident, KTLO, a shift in priorities). Every milestone's forecast reads the same entries, and they carry over to the next milestone. We chose this because what reduces capacity happens to the team, not to one piece of its work: someone on leave is away from all of it, and an EM shouldn't have to type the same absence again for each milestone.

## Considered Options

- **Capacity per milestone**, which would let an EM split a team between two milestones running at once. Not chosen: the team's absences would be typed again for each milestone, and the split would be a guess the forecast can't check.

## Consequences

- Two milestones running at once each forecast with the whole team's capacity. An EM splitting a team between them should give each a pace that fits its share, or point comparable work at work done at that share.
- Moving capacity to the milestone later means a migration of every team's stored entries, and deciding which milestone each one belongs to.
